Help in Getting Your

G2 in 3 Easy Steps

Our MTO-approved curriculum combines flexible classroom learning with personalized in-car coaching.

See All Course Details
01

In-class or online Drivers Ed

5-hour days to help prepare for your G1. In the 4 classes, you will learn the rules of the road, defensive driving techniques, fines & penalties, and more as required by the MTO.

02

In-vehicle Lessons

After successfully completing the course and passing your G1 written exam, get 10 hours of one-on-one in-car training with a certified driving instructor.

03

Getting certified

After completing steps 1 & 2 you are ready to receive your certification. This allows you to get your G2 after only 8 months of your G1, and is recognized by the auto insurance industry for potential discounts.
